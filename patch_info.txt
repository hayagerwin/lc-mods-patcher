=== [v4.2.0] - 2026-08-27 15:35 PHT ===
* Synchronized Descent Scene Loading Gates (MoonRouteBuffer v1.4.0): Fixed the critical desync bug where the host's fast PC loaded the moon terrain and generated the dungeon while slower PCs were still loading the additive scene and saw only outer space.
* Deep Asset Pre-Warming in Orbit: The client now recursively touches and pre-loads all level enemy prefabs, outside monsters, scrap props, and LethalLevelLoader streaming AssetBundles into memory during orbit transit before signaling readiness.
* Dual-Phase Client/Host Handshake: Added `MSG_SCENE_LOADED` network verification. The host's descent sequence now strictly pauses in `OpenShipDoors` until EVERY connected client has finished loading the additive terrain scene.
* Deferred Client Floor Generation Guard: If a client receives a floor generation RPC while the scene is still finishing loading, `GenerateNewFloor` automatically defers until `dungeonGenerator` is instantiated, preventing null errors and desynced dungeon generation.

=== [v4.1.0] - 2026-08-27 15:05 PHT ===
* Recalibrated Moon Route Asset Caching & Transit Buffer (MoonRouteBuffer v1.3.0): Fixed the bug where the host immediately pulling the lever after routing to a new moon overwhelmed slower PCs.
* Multi-Stage Client Asset Verification: Every client now asynchronously waits for custom moon streaming AssetBundles (LethalLevelLoader) and visual meshes to finish loading, and stabilizes disk I/O and garbage collection before reporting readiness.
* Configurable Transit Stabilization Buffer: Added a 3-second minimum post-arrival transit buffer (`MinTransitBufferSeconds = 3.0s`) to give slower PCs breathing room to decompress assets and warm up shaders.
* Multi-Point Anti-Race Lever Guards: Hard prefix interceptors on `PullLever`, `StartGame`, `StartGameServerRpc`, and `StartMatchLever.Update` guarantee the start match lever cannot be pulled or triggered until all connected players have verified asset readiness.
* Real-Time Caching Progress & HUD Feedback: Dynamic lever hover tip displays `[ Caching Moon Assets (X/Y)... ]` and notifies the lobby when all crew members are cached and ready to land.

=== [v4.0.1] - 2026-08-27 09:46 PHT ===
* Permanent Microphone Un-Mute on Custom Moons: Disabled artificial landing mute by default (`EnableLandingGuard = false`) and added an enforced 5-second maximum failsafe watchdog alongside DunGen level generation hooks. Guarantees your microphone is never left in a muted state on custom moons (Zenit, PsychSanctum, E-Gypt, etc.).

=== [v4.0.0] - 2026-08-27 09:13 PHT ===
* Critical VoIP Disconnect Auto-Heal in LandingVoiceGuard (v3.9.0): Hooked player connect/disconnect events to immediately re-evaluate and un-mute Dissonance's `VoiceBroadcastTrigger`. Fixes the bug where teammates couldn't hear your voice after a player abruptly dropped from the lobby.
* Spectator 3D AudioListener Dynamic Camera Binding: When dead and spectating, the AudioListener now dynamically attaches directly to the spectated teammate's camera, eliminating distance attenuation so alive teammates can always be heard at full clarity.
* Orphaned Disconnected Slot Voice Guard: Patched `StartOfRound.UpdatePlayerVoiceEffects` to ignore empty player slots, stopping the 2-second error loop and preventing unnecessary voice component resets.
* Transmission Keep-Alive Watchdog: Added a background heartbeat to guarantee outgoing microphone transmission channels never get stuck in a closed state.
